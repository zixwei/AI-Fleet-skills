Art style formulas

The formula that each of the 100 art-style shaders in atlas/art/<id>.js actually computes. The mathematics column states each computation in standard notation: H is the Heaviside step, ⌊x⌋ the floor, {x} the fractional part, h a hash, η value noise, and Sa,b(x) = 3s² − 2s³ with s = min(1, max(0, (x − a)/(b − a))) the smooth step from a to b. The code-notation column keeps the shader's own terms. Inputs: C scene colour, D depth, N surface normal, W world position, B beat times, t time, X renders at earlier times. Every style is a pure function of these inputs (no random numbers, no clock, no memory of the previous frame). Formulas were read from the code by 4 Claude Sonnet agents, 25 styles each; constants are the ones in the code.
