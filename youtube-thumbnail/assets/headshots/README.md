Drop headshot photos of yourself here (PNG or JPG).

```
.claude/skills/youtube-thumbnail/assets/headshots/
```

These are uploaded to the image engine (Higgsfield Nano Banana Pro, or the Gemini fallback) as reference images when generating thumbnails. The AI uses your likeness to composite you into the thumbnail.

Tips for best results:
- Clear, well-lit face
- Multiple angles and expressions give more options — the skill picks the expression that matches each concept
- Shoulders-up or waist-up framing works best
- Transparent or simple backgrounds are ideal

If you cloned this repo and see someone else's headshots here, delete them and add your own.
