## Quality Check

Before posting, ask:
1. Would this get lost in a feed? → Needs a stronger hook
2. Is the insight generic? → Needs your specific angle
3. Could any account post this? → Needs more personality

## Engagement Guidelines

- Respond to every comment (especially under 1K followers)
- Match the depth of the comment
- Ask follow-up questions
- Never be defensive
