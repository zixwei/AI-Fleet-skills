## What We Track

- **Saves/bookmarks** — content worth returning to
- **Reply-to-like ratio** — conversation vs. passive consumption
- **Thread continuation rate** — are people reading the whole thing
- **Comments with follow-up questions** — genuine engagement
- **Follower retention** — are people staying after following
- **Cross-platform referrals** — did a Reddit post drive Bluesky follows?
